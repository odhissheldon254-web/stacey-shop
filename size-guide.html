Size Guide

Charts to get you close, and a person to get you exact. If you are between sizes, message Stacey — she can measure the actual item instead of quoting a generic chart.

Measure your foot first

Two minutes here saves an exchange later. Do this in the evening, when your feet are at their largest.

  • Put a sheet of paper flat against a wall and stand on it with your heel touching the wall.
  • Mark the paper at the tip of your longest toe — which is not always the big toe.
  • Measure from the wall edge to that mark in centimetres.
  • Measure both feet and use the larger one. Most people have a slight difference.
