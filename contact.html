What to message about

  • Placing an order. Build your bag on the site first — the checkout button sends the whole list through in one message so nothing gets garbled.
  • Sizing. Send the item and your measurement and Stacey will check the actual pair. Start with the size guide.
  • Delivery to your area. If your town is not in the rate table, ask and she will quote the courier fee up front.
  • Sourcing a specific piece. Send a photo of what you are after. A good share of the collection started as a customer request.
  • A problem with an order. Message within 48 hours of delivery with a photo — see the FAQs for how returns work.
