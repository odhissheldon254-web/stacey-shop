How Ordering Works

Simple, personal, and safe — every order is confirmed with Stacey before you pay a shilling.

1

Browse & add to bag

Pick your favourites from the catalog and add them to your order bag. No account needed.

2

Confirm on WhatsApp

Your order opens as a WhatsApp message to Stacey, who confirms availability, sizes, and your delivery details.

3

Pay via M-Pesa

Pay only after Stacey confirms your order, to the Tacey Collections paybill — the numbers are published here, with the total agreed upfront.

4

Delivery or pickup

Doorstep delivery across Kenya (fees shown at checkout) or free self-pickup. Wrong item or size? Message Stacey within 48 hours to arrange an exchange.
